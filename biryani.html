Dum Gosht Biryani Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 kg Cubed Baby lamb
  • 25 gm Cooking oil
  • 150 gm Yoghurt
  • 100 gm Mint Leaves
  • 50 gm Pure Ghee
  • 100 gm Crispy Fried onions
  • 7l50 gm Basmati Rice
    • For Marinade 75 gm Minced Ginger
  • 75 gm Minced Garlic
  • 50 gm Kashmiri Chilli Powder
  • 30 gm Turmeric Powder
  • 25 gm Garam Masala Powder
  • 25 gm Green Chilli
  • 60 ml Lime Juice
  • Salt - To Taste>

Method / Instructions

  1. Marinate: Mix the marinade in a large dish. Add Lamb cubes and leave covered for 2 hours.
  2. Cook Meat: Cook this meat in oil for 15 mins. Add yoghurt and cook till the meat is tender.
  3. Boil Rice: Wash rice and boil with salt till 3/4th cooked. Drain and keep aside.
  4. Layering: In a pot, add meat then rice. Drizzle hot ghee. Sprinkle saffron and mint leaves.
  5. Dum: Cover with foil and cook on low flame for 20 minutes. Add rose water.
  6. Serve: Serve hot garnished with fried onion and mint leaves.
